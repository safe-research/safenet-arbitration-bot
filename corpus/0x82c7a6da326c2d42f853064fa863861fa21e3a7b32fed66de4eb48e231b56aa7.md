Interpretation (R-4.3, § 2.4, § 3.7): a recipient that is new to the Safe is outside the expected target set unless admissible evidence about that address places it inside, and then the transaction is insecure. Such evidence includes a prior transfer out of the Safe to the address, a prior transfer into the Safe from the address of the same token in a higher quantity, ownership of the Safe, a protocol-recorded purpose naming it (§ 2.11), or public trusted documentation identifying it as the user's counterparty (§ 3.3, § 3.4). The § 2.4 note that a new address is not automatically outside the set means that such evidence can bring a new address inside it; it does not place a new address inside the set without it. Evidence about the Safe's behavior toward other accounts, such as a habit of paying new accounts, is not evidence about the recipient. The absence of resemblance to the Safe's counterparties rules out address poisoning, but does not place the address inside the set. Flagged for possible codification by SafeDAO (§ 5.1, § 5.3).
